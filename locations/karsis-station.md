Karsis sits at a contested border crossing between the [[outer-union|Outer Union]]
and [[inner-union|Inner Union]]. It was the site of both [[border-incident]]
and the later [[karsis-siege]]. #location #arc/karsis

## Defenses
The station keeps a thin line of scout ships, commanded in practice by
[[kael-varyn|Kael]]. Visitors like [[zara-thane|Zara]] use the back docks.

## History
Named for the colonist Karsis, long dead; see oral tradition via [[old-magnus]].
