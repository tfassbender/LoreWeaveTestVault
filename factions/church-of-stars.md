The Church is led by [[selene-priestess|Selene]] from the
[[starlight-sanctuary]]. Its doctrines are compiled in the
[[magic-system|System of Stars]] and its relics include the
[[prophecy-shard]]. #faction/church

The Church treats the [[void-crystal]] as heresy material and refuses to
acknowledge its existence publicly. #faction
