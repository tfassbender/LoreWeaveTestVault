The System of Stars teaches that light carries intent: stars communicate by
pulse patterns that the [[church-of-stars|Church]] decodes as prophecy. The
[[prophecy-shard]] is a mnemonic device, not a conduit. The
[[void-crystal]] is, according to Church doctrine, forbidden — its pulse
patterns are said to originate from outside the light. #rule #doctrine

The related material of [[the-void]] is mentioned in the scriptures but
considered apocryphal. #arc/karsis
